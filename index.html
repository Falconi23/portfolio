    About

    I'm a Control Engineer and MSc student at Sapienza University of Rome, drawn to the layer where estimation, guidance, and learning meet — the control loops and models that let a system act reliably despite what it can't measure directly. That's taken shape as a first-author paper on deep RL for autonomous debris capture (IEEE/IFAC CoDIT 2026), a 20-state Extended Kalman Filter flying on the Sapienza Flight Team's VTOL autopilot, and now a research internship with the RAINBOW Team (IRISA-Inria, CNRS) in Rennes, on world models for aerial robots.

    Outside engineering, I'm highly active and adventure-driven: I compete in sports, explore the outdoors, and pursue continuous learning — curiosity keeps me up to date on new technologies, research, and tools.
